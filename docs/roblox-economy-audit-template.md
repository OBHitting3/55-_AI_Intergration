## Anti-exploit checklist

- Receipt validation exists for developer products.
- Purchases are granted once per receipt.
- Currency rewards have server-side checks.
- Trade or transfer systems prevent duplicate grants.
- Unusual earning speed is flagged.
- Admin/test grants are separated from live economy data.
