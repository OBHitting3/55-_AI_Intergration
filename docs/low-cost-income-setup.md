# Low-cost income setup: Roblox, SuperbulletAI, Fiverr, Gumroad, and videos

## Decision summary

Do not spend heavily on a full Roblox launch yet. The strongest low-cost path is to sell small, proof-backed services and digital products built from the Palm Luxe Tycoon work, then use those sales to decide whether to keep investing in the game.

This setup treats the current Roblox project as a portfolio asset and production engine, not as the only income source.

## What was checked

- The repo already contains a Palm Luxe Tycoon Roblox economy system with pricing, anti-exploit, asset transfer, simulation, and direct-purchase monetization notes.
- Roblox monetization is real but slower: DevEx depends on earned Robux, eligibility, and game/player traction.
- Fiverr is fast to list services, but seller earnings are reduced by platform fees; it is best used as a lead source.
- Gumroad has no monthly fee and is better for reusable files, templates, prompt packs, videos, and checklists.
- YouTube monetization should not be treated as immediate income because Partner Program thresholds require subscriber and watch/view volume.
- SuperbulletAI appears useful as a Roblox production accelerator, but output should be reviewed and tested before being sold or shipped.

## Verified channel facts

| Channel | Verified fact | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roblox | Developer products and game passes use `MarketplaceService`; developer products require `ProcessReceipt` validation. Cross-experience sales are disabled starting May 30, 2026. | Build monetization inside the experience and test purchase receipt handling before expecting revenue. |
| Roblox DevEx | DevEx is the official cash-out path for earned Robux. Roblox announced a higher rate for eligible in-game spend from age-checked 18+ US users starting June 8, 2026. | Roblox can become meaningful, but it is not the fastest cash path unless the game already has demand. |
| Fiverr | Public fee references and Fiverr help pages indicate sellers generally keep 80% of order value, before payout/currency costs. | Price services with the fee included. A $50 order should be treated like roughly $40 before other costs. |
| Gumroad | Gumroad's pricing page lists 10% + $0.50 for direct sales and 30% for Discover marketplace sales. | Good for no-monthly-cost products, but low-priced products lose margin to fixed fees. |
| YouTube | YouTube Help lists full Partner Program thresholds at 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 valid public watch hours or 10 million Shorts views. | Use YouTube as marketing, not the first revenue engine. |
| SuperbulletAI | SuperbulletAI documentation positions it as a Roblox AI game builder and plugin workflow with natural-language generation. | Use it to make prototypes faster, but keep human QA and ownership of final assets. |

## Offer ladder

Start with offers that require no inventory, no ads, and no paid tooling.

### Offer 1: Fiverr service - Roblox economy teardown

**Gig title:** I will review your Roblox game economy and give a monetization improvement plan

**Best first package:**
- Basic: $35 for one-page review of currency, sinks, sources, and pricing.
- Standard: $75 for review plus pass/developer-product recommendations.
- Premium: $150 for review plus a 10-item economy table and anti-exploit checklist.

**Delivery file:** PDF or Markdown report.

**Proof to use:** Palm Luxe Tycoon has a dynamic pricing engine, currency sinks/sources, anti-exploit checks, and simulation tooling.

**Do not promise:** guaranteed revenue, guaranteed DAU, guaranteed Robux.

### Offer 2: Gumroad product - Roblox economy starter kit

**Product title:** Roblox Tycoon Economy Starter Kit

**Format:**
- `README` explaining sources, sinks, pricing, and passes.
- Spreadsheet template for currency flow and item pricing.
- Checklist for developer products, passes, receipts, and anti-exploit review.
- Optional video walkthrough hosted privately or delivered as a file.

**Recommended starter price:** $9 to $19. Avoid very low prices because Gumroad has fixed per-transaction fees.

**Positioning:** "A practical planning kit for Roblox creators who need a safer in-game economy before adding monetization."

### Offer 3: Gumroad product - SuperbulletAI prompt pack for Roblox creators

**Product title:** SuperbulletAI Prompt Pack for Roblox Tycoon Systems

**Format:**
- Prompt templates for economy design, UI, shop systems, anti-exploit checks, and QA.
- "Reference path" prompting examples.
- QA checklist that explains what must be tested in Roblox Studio after generation.

**Important rule:** Do not sell raw AI output as final production code. Sell the workflow, prompts, review checklist, and edited examples.

### Offer 4: Video micro-product

**Product title:** Build a Roblox Tycoon Economy Without Lootboxes

**Format:** 20-40 minute screen-recorded walkthrough plus templates.

**Where to sell first:** Gumroad direct link. Use YouTube Shorts or free clips only to drive attention.

## Production process

1. Pick one narrow asset to produce.
2. Turn it into a sellable template, checklist, or service deliverable.
3. Record one short proof video showing the template or workflow.
4. Publish on Gumroad.
5. Publish one Fiverr gig that solves the same problem as a custom service.
6. Post short clips that point to the Gumroad product and Fiverr gig.
7. Track clicks, questions, favorites, and sales.
8. Improve the offer only when real user behavior shows confusion or demand.

## Spend control rules

- Spend $0 on ads until at least one product or service sells organically.
- Use free recording, editing, and document tools first.
- Do not buy Roblox assets until a specific deliverable requires them.
- Do not pay for a marketplace listing before the Gumroad/Fiverr copy is live.
- Do not expand to three products at once. Publish one product and one service first.

## Decision gates

Use these gates to decide whether an idea is right or wrong:

| Signal | Meaning | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People ask questions but do not buy | Offer may be interesting but unclear | Rewrite title, thumbnail, and first paragraph |
| Fiverr gets impressions but no clicks | Gig positioning is weak | Change title and first image |
| Clicks happen but no purchases | Price, proof, or deliverable is weak | Add sample pages/video and simplify package |
| First sale happens | Demand exists | Improve delivery quality and ask what almost stopped them |
| No engagement after multiple posts/listings | Market signal is weak | Stop expanding that offer and test a narrower one |

## Audit log

- Decision: do not make Roblox DevEx the first income dependency.
  - Reason: it requires game traction and payout eligibility.
- Decision: use Fiverr for custom service discovery.
  - Reason: it can create buyer conversations quickly, but fees mean pricing must be padded.
- Decision: use Gumroad for reusable templates and video products.
  - Reason: no monthly platform fee and direct links work well for small digital products.
- Decision: use SuperbulletAI as a production accelerator only.
  - Reason: AI-generated Roblox work still needs review, testing, and ownership before selling.
- Decision: use YouTube as marketing first.
  - Reason: Partner Program thresholds make it a slower direct-revenue path.
