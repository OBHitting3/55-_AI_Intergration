HUNTLEY HOLDINGS, LP

Limited Partnership Agreement
Effective Date: January 15, 2009
Last Amended: November 8, 2022

SECTION 1. FORMATION

Huntley Holdings, LP is a Delaware limited partnership formed under the Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act.

SECTION 2. PARTNERS

2.1 General Partner: Huntley Management, LLC (a Delaware limited liability company, wholly owned by Margaret Anne Huntley).

2.2 Limited Partners, as of the November 8, 2022 amendment, hold the following percentage interests:
  - The Huntley Grandchildren's Trust: 45%
  - Robert J. Huntley III, individually: 20%
  - Catherine Huntley-Marsh, individually: 20%
  - Edward L. Huntley, individually: 15%

SECTION 3. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

The initial capital contribution at formation in 2009 totaled $42,000,000, consisting of marketable securities and a 12% membership interest in Coastal Vineyard Estates, LLC.

SECTION 4. DISTRIBUTIONS

4.1 Available cash shall be distributed quarterly in proportion to each partner's percentage interest.

4.2 No distribution shall be made if, after giving effect to the distribution, the Partnership would be unable to meet its liabilities as they become due in the ordinary course of business.

SECTION 5. TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS

No partner may transfer all or any portion of its interest in the Partnership without the prior written consent of the General Partner, which consent may be withheld in the General Partner's sole and absolute discretion.

SECTION 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

All information concerning the Partnership, its assets, and its partners shall be held in strict confidence. Disclosure to outside counsel, accountants, and tax advisors is permitted subject to a written confidentiality agreement.

SECTION 7. DISSOLUTION

The Partnership shall continue until December 31, 2049, unless earlier dissolved by unanimous written consent of all partners or by operation of law.

SECTION 8. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

EXECUTED as of November 8, 2022.

Margaret Anne Huntley, sole member of Huntley Management, LLC
Robert J. Huntley III, Limited Partner
Catherine Huntley-Marsh, Limited Partner
Edward L. Huntley, Limited Partner
