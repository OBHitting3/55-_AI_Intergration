THE HUNTLEY GRANDCHILDREN'S TRUST

Original Indenture dated April 12, 1994
Amended and Restated June 3, 2017

ARTICLE I — ESTABLISHMENT

This Trust is established by Margaret Anne Huntley (the "Settlor") for the benefit of her grandchildren and their descendants, to be administered by Wilshire Trust Company, N.A. (the "Trustee") pursuant to the terms set forth herein.

ARTICLE II — BENEFICIARIES

Section 2.01. As of the original Indenture dated April 12, 1994, the named beneficiaries were Robert J. Huntley III, Catherine Huntley-Marsh, and Edward L. Huntley, in equal one-third shares.

Section 2.02. Pursuant to the 2017 Amendment, the beneficiary class was expanded to include all lineal descendants of the Settlor born prior to December 31, 2025. The 2017 Amendment further provides that distributions to Edward L. Huntley shall be reduced to a one-fifth share, with the remaining four-fifths to be distributed pro rata among the other named beneficiaries and their descendants.

Section 2.03. Distributions to any beneficiary under the age of twenty-five (25) shall be held in custodianship and applied solely to the beneficiary's health, education, maintenance, and support (the "HEMS standard").

ARTICLE III — TRUSTEE POWERS

The Trustee shall have full discretion to invest, reinvest, and manage Trust assets, subject to the prudent investor rule and to the directives of any Investment Direction Advisor appointed under Article VI.

ARTICLE IV — DISTRIBUTIONS

Section 4.01. The Trustee shall make annual distributions of net income to the beneficiaries in accordance with the share percentages set forth in Section 2.02.

Section 4.02. Principal distributions may be made at the discretion of the Trustee upon written request of a beneficiary, subject to the HEMS standard for beneficiaries under twenty-five (25).

ARTICLE V — SUCCESSOR TRUSTEE

Upon the resignation, removal, or incapacity of Wilshire Trust Company, N.A., the successor trustee shall be Pacific Coast Fiduciary, LLC, of Los Angeles, California.

ARTICLE VI — INVESTMENT DIRECTION ADVISOR

The Settlor appoints her son, Robert J. Huntley III, as the Investment Direction Advisor for the Trust. The Advisor may direct the Trustee with respect to the management of Trust investments, including the retention or sale of any concentrated position in Huntley Holdings, LP.

ARTICLE VII — GOVERNING LAW

This Trust shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

SIGNED this 3rd day of June, 2017.

Margaret Anne Huntley, Settlor
Wilshire Trust Company, N.A., by its authorized officer
